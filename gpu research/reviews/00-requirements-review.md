1. **Top 5 conflates incomparable stacks and has no answer for "don't self-host".** Ranking five (model+harness+GPU+provider) combos can crowd out the likely real answer: use a hosted API. Fix: make the decision verdict (self-host vs. API vs. managed per-second inference like Replicate/fal/Modal) the first line of `RECOMMENDATIONS.md`, and require at least 1–2 of the five slots to be non-self-host baselines.

2. **The cost formula is wrong or underspecified.** Dividing by utilisation treats cost as if the GPU is idle-billed, but it omits cold start and model load, failed or discarded generations (retry rate), multi-GPU/sharded runs, and batching. Fix: add an "effective cost per *accepted* video" term (price × seconds/3600 × (1/utilisation) × (1/acceptance rate)), and model load as a per-job amortised cost with explicit warm vs. cold scenarios.

3. **Missing cost components.** There's no always-on vs. scale-to-zero distinction, no weights storage (~50–100+ GB), egress, orchestration/queueing, ops engineering time, or monitoring. Fix: add a TCO table (GPU, storage, egress, idle floor, engineer hours at a stated INR rate), and show a "serverless per-second" column alongside "dedicated VM".

4. **S1–S3 are too narrow and partly undefined.** S2 has no duration or fps, S3 has no fps, and there's no steps/CFG setting, seed policy, or quality-equivalence spec. Without a quality bar, cost across models is incomparable (distilled 4-step vs. 50-step). Fix: define each scenario fully (duration, fps, resolution, steps, T2V vs. I2V), and require a quality tier label per model plus a stated judgement method (e.g. VBench score or side-by-side), noting that benchmarks may not exist.

5. **"Measured generation time" is mostly unobtainable under the no-spend rule.** Published benchmarks are sparse, vary by step count, attention kernel, offload and resolution, and are often vendor-reported. Scaling across GPUs "as inference" is unreliable (memory bandwidth vs. FLOPs, FP8 support, NVLink). Fix: define a scaling method with an error band (e.g. ±30–50%), reject results that don't state steps/resolution/precision, and make the proposed paid benchmark run a mandatory follow-up for the top 3 before any commitment.

6. **API baseline is under-specified.** FR4 asks for a baseline but doesn't name providers or models (Veo, Sora, Runway, Kling, Luma, Pika, Hailuo/MiniMax, Seedance, fal.ai- and Replicate-hosted open models). Hosted open-weights endpoints are the most relevant comparison, because they share the same model but remove the ops burden. Fix: enumerate at least 6 API providers with price per second at matching resolution, dated, and include hosted-open-model pricing as a separate row.

7. **The Indian-provider framing risks biasing the answer.** Many Indian providers don't list H100/H200/B200 prices publicly, Indian GPU availability for multi-GPU nodes is limited, and the doc doesn't say how "not publicly listed" options get ranked or whether they can be in the top 5 at all. Fix: add an eligibility rule (must have a verifiable public price or a dated quote, otherwise excluded from ranking), include GST (18%), import and forex effects, and a stated provider availability/quota check.

8. **GPU coverage is unspecified.** No GPU list is given, so mapping may skew toward the H100. Fix: fix the candidate set up front (RTX 4090/5090, L40S, A100 40/80, H100, H200, B200, and AMD MI300X, which matters because of ROCm support gaps in ComfyUI/diffusers pipelines), and flag consumer-GPU terms-of-service limits (Vast/RunPod community cloud vs. secure cloud, reliability and data-privacy implications).

9. **Licensing and operational risk are thin.** "Flag commercial-use restrictions" is out-of-scope hand-waving: some licences (Tencent, LTX, Mochi variants) carry territory, revenue or user-count thresholds, and some exclude the EU, UK or South Korea. There's also no content-safety or misuse liability, model-weight provenance, or harness maturity check. Fix: add a licence matrix (commercial use, revenue cap, territory, output ownership, attribution) as a WP1 gate, and a risk column for harness stability and maintenance activity (last commit, open issues).

10. **Process requirements are partly untestable, and the rubric is hard to pass.** "Non-obvious", "no open objection that changes the ranking" and "independent" reviewers (same `claude -p` model family, same priors, likely the same web sources) can't be verified, and the quality bar's rule 5 ("cheapest disproving experiment") for every insight is heavy for a 10-minute read. The deadline is also undefined: WP1–WP6 are sequential with gates, but the doc has no time budget. Fix: replace "independent" with a concrete protocol (a reviewer given the raw sources only, not the draft; at least one reviewer using a different model or provider), make "changes the ranking" mechanical (re-run `cost_model.py` with the objection's value and check whether the top-5 order flips), and set a time box per WP plus a one-page decision summary to meet the 10-minute-read goal.
