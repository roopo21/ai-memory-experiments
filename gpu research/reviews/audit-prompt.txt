# Recommendations: GPUs and cost to self-host a video generation model

Prepared 2026-11-20 for the Asana task "Figure out GPUs and cost to self-host a video gen model". Numbers come from `cost_model.py` (re-run it to check any figure). Evidence tags: F = read on a primary page, V = vendor or aggregator claim, I = inference, O = assumption.

## Verdict

**Do not self-host yet. Use a hosted open-weights endpoint now, a closed API for hero shots, and spend about Rs10-12k on one benchmark before committing to any GPU.** At 500 to 5,000 accepted 5 s / 720p clips a month, hosted options cost about $0.35-0.60 per accepted clip, and the same model on a rented GPU **never breaks even with its hosted version within 20,000 clips a month** once about $215 a month of engineer time is counted. Self-hosting only starts to win above roughly 3,300 clips a month, and only with a distilled model whose speed is unverified.

### Dropped, and why
- **Wan 2.2 A14B at full 40 steps, anywhere**: $2.27 per accepted clip on RunPod, $3.02 on E2E, $4.79 on Yotta. Never competitive with its own hosted price ($0.73).
- **Wan 2.2 A14B on fal.ai**: the page says the model is removed on 2027-01-04, about six weeks away.
- **LTX-2.x self-host** (H100 or RTX 5090): no primary timing and no official VRAM figure, and the licence needs a $10M revenue check and a visible watermark. Revisit after the benchmark; it is the one model that adds native audio.
- **Every Indian provider** (E2E, Yotta, Neysa, JarvisLabs) and the IndiaAI portal: 1.2-3x the RunPod H100 price ($2.89 secure; $1.99 community), or an unverifiable rate card. GST is excluded everywhere in the model; a registered business can usually claim it back.
- **Veo 3.1 Lite at $0.03-0.05/s**: only aggregators list it; Google's pages were unreadable.

## What drives the cost (the non-obvious part)

1. **Rent, ops time and acceptance rate dominate. Generation time barely matters.** At 500-5,000 clips a month one rented box covers the volume, so a clip costs about (monthly rent + engineer time) / volume. The three weakest timings (LTX, Hunyuan at 720p, distilled Wan) change the answer less than assumptions about acceptance. So the benchmark should measure acceptance first and speed second.
2. **Acceptance is an opinion in this model.** Priors range from 35% (Wan 5B) to 85% (Veo). Re-running every option at one shared 50% moves hosted Wan 5B to the top ($0.30) and hosted Veo to $1.00. Only the blind test settles this.
3. **Utilisation follows volume.** At 500 clips a month a 4090 is busy about 11% of the time, which makes a dedicated box 2-5x pricier per clip than a naive 60% utilisation assumption suggests.

## The one experiment (about Rs10-12k, 1-2 engineer-days)

One prompt set, 50 short prompts at 1280x720, 121 frames, 24 fps, fixed seed, run on eight setups with two blind raters scoring accept/reject:
- RunPod H100 PCIe: Wan 2.2 A14B + Lightning 4-step; HunyuanVideo 1.5 720p step-distilled; LTX-2.5 fp8.
- RunPod RTX 4090: HunyuanVideo 1.5 with offload; Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B with official settings.
- Hosted: fal Wan 5B, Hailuo 2.3, Veo 3.1 Fast silent.
Record wall time including model load, peak VRAM and accept rate. Expected spend about $45 (H100) + $9 (4090) + $47 (APIs). Feed the results into `cost_model.py` and the top 5 settles.
